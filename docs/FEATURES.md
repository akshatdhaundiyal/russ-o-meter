# Russ-O-Meter Feature Specification

A detailed breakdown of all features and mechanics in the Russ-O-Meter platform.

---

## 1. 🚀 Hype Engine
- **Pitch Hyper-Inflation**: Rewrites any mundane side-project idea into an absurd, buzzword-dense VC pitch.
- **Valuation Counter**: Animated counting engine starting at $1B with incremental multipliers and comma additions.
- **Add a Comma ($100M)**: Multiplies valuation by 10x per click; reaching 3 commas triggers the *Three Commas Club* celebration modal with custom confetti and fanfare.
- **Surprise Me (🎲)**: Loads one of 20+ hilarious pre-configured mundane ideas (*"A smart Bluetooth toothpick dispenser"*, *"An alarm clock that sounds like seagulls"*, etc.).
